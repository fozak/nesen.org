About NESEN

Built in New England.
Referral-first.
Free to join.

NESEN — New England Science and Entrepreneurship Network — is an independent community organization formed in Massachusetts in 2025 to support US entrepreneurship, accelerate innovation, and build a strong, trusted networking community across New England. Our mission is simple: connect the right people, create the right introductions, and let relationships do the rest.

The community consists of entrepreneurs, startup founders, executives, and scientists with entrepreneurial intentions — people who grow through trusted relationships rather than cold outreach.

Weekly meetings

Every Thursday at 6:30 PM in Cambridge MA. A structured 90-minute format that turns attendance into referrals — always.

Referral-first format

Every meeting has one purpose: warm introductions. Named, specific referrals from people accountable to the room — not ambient goodwill.

Pitch segment

One member pitches each week. The room responds with honest feedback and specific introductions. For founders, it is the fastest way to compress months of trial and error into a single focused session.

Free to attend

No membership fee. The investment is your time, your referrals, and your presence. A seat in NESEN is an active relationship — not a passive credential.
